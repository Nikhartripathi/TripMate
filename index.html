About TripMate

TripMate is an intelligent travel recommendation system built to simplify the way you explore the world. Instead of spending hours researching destinations, reading reviews, and budgeting manually, our AI does the heavy lifting for you.

By analyzing your preferences—such as location, interests, duration, and budget—TripMate curates a complete, tailored travel experience so you can focus on making memories.
